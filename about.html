watch closely.

For most of history, people learned a trade by working beside someone who already knew it. Our machines have never had that chance.

We built machines that fly,think,and leave the planet.

Yet ask one to fold a shirt or fit two parts together, and it still struggles. People learned that kind of work by watching it done, and machines have had nothing to watch.

The internet taught machines to talk.Nothing has taught them to work.

That’s what Farasa is for. We record real work first-person, track the hands, log the machines on the same job, and label every frame. When you need something we haven’t recorded, we build it as a custom dataset to your spec.

For five thousand years, we built machines to carry us. Now we’re teaching them to work beside us.
