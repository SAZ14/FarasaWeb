Watch closely.

For most of history, people learned a trade by working next to someone who already knew it.

We built machines that fly,think,and leave the planet.

The internet taught machines to talk.Nothing has taught them to work.

Language models learned from billions of written words. A robot that lays brick or assembles a motor needs a different kind of record: the work itself, filmed from where the worker stands.

That’s what Farasa builds. We film real shifts on construction sites and factory floors, log the machines on the same job, and label every frame.

We’ve spent five thousand years building machines to carry us. Now we’re teaching them to work alongside us.
