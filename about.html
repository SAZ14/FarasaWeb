Watch closely.

For most of history, people learned a trade by working beside someone who already knew it. Our machines have never had that chance.

We built machines that fly,think,and leave the planet.

The internet taught machines to talk.Nothing has taught them to work.

Language models learned to talk by reading billions of words. A robot that lays brick or assembles a motor can’t learn that way. It has to see the work itself, from where the worker stands.

That’s what Farasa records. We film real shifts on construction sites and factory floors, log the machines working the same job, and label every frame.

For five thousand years, we built machines to carry us. Now we’re teaching them to work beside us.
