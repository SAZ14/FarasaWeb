Watch closely.

For most of history, the way to learn a trade was to stand beside someone who knew it. You watched their hands, then you tried it yourself.

We built machines that fly,think,and leave the planet.

The internet taught machines to talk.Nothing has taught them to work.

Language models learned from billions of written words. A robot that lays brick or assembles a motor needs something different: a record of the work itself, seen from where the worker stands.

Farasa makes that record. We capture first-person video on construction sites and factory floors, log the machines working on the same job, and label every frame.

A rider and a B-2 in the same sky: five thousand years of machines built to carry us. The next ones have to learn the work itself, and they will learn it from us.
