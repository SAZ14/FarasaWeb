watch closely.

For most of history, people learned a trade by working beside someone who already knew it. Our machines have never had that chance.

We built machines that fly,think,and leave the planet.

The internet taught machines to talk.Nothing has taught them to work.

Language models learned to talk by reading billions of words. A robot that folds laundry or assembles a motor can’t learn that way. It has to see the work itself, from where the person doing it stands.

That’s what Farasa records: real work as it happens, seen first-person, with the hands tracked, the touch measured, and every frame labelled. When you need something we haven’t recorded, we build it, as a custom dataset to your spec.

For five thousand years, we built machines to carry us. Now we’re teaching them to work beside us.
