// physical data for ai

the future is
in your hands.


        
Farasa records how people work in the physical world: what they see, how their hands move, what they touch, and what the machines beside them do. Every frame labelled, so robots can learn real work.

Every sentence ever typed became training data. Almost none of the work done with hands did. That’s where we come in.

what we capture

      

        

          
streams01

          

          
First-person video, hand pose, force, and motion, every stream on one clock.

        

        

          
space02

          

          
Depth and position: where the hands, tools, and objects are, frame by frame.

        

        

          
labels03

          

          
Pose, objects, contact, and actions, reviewed twice before anything ships.
