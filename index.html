The problem

Most physical AI data never touches the real world

Models trained for physical work are still learning from simulation, staged lab demonstrations, or datasets that are too clean to transfer. Real job sites are unstructured: cluttered, dynamic, and full of long, multi-step tasks that synthetic data can't reproduce.

Simulated

Physics engines miss the friction, deformation, and unpredictability of real materials and tools.

Lab-based

Controlled environments strip out the clutter, occlusion, and improvisation that define real work.

Too clean

Short, scripted clips can't teach the long-horizon reasoning that physical tasks demand.
